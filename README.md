#### Question 1: How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating "user stories" help with this?

<p>Interpreting user needs and implementing them into programs starts with listening to user feedback and complaints actively, then transforming that feedback into technical language that the development team can work with. User stories help with this by having a log of every complaint or request given to the team. It allows the team to think of the person behind the program, as well as prioritize the issues that are recurring.</p>

#### Question 2: How do I approach developing programs? what Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work? 

<p>I approach developing programs as a brute force method. I work until I hit a roadblock and then spend the rest of my time working on figuring out an answer to the question. I think one Agile process I hope to incorporate into my development process is sprints. Breaking down my process into short sprints focusing on one section will not only help with procrastination, but allow me to take time away from the problem and figure out what exactly is going wrong or where my roadblock is.</p>

#### Question 3: What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

<p>To be a good team member in software development, you must be able to adapt to change, learn from your mistakes, and communicate effectively. By adapting to change, you open yourself up to new ideas and thought process you may have never discovered. Learning from your mistakes allows you to be able to acknowledge them in the future before they become prominent issues. Communicating effectively with your team members allows your thoughts and ideas to be spread among the team without any confusion or misinterpretation.  </p>
